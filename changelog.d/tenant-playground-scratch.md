- **Actuator playground scratch follows the active scope partition** — sessions, ADF drafts, and live input payloads now land under `active/shared/tmp/<tier>/<tenant|shared>/actuator-playground/` instead of the legacy unpartitioned `active/shared/tmp/actuator-playground/` root, so confidential tenant trials (browser discovery for internal approval flows, etc.) stay on that tenant's floor. Protected-tier I/O and child actuator spawns run under the tenant-bound `actuator_playground_worker` role (same pattern as `perception_media_worker`); `buildSafeExecEnv` now also mirrors a tenant-bound ALS assumption into the child's `KYBERION_TENANT` so `${KYBERION_TENANT}` grants expand after spawn. `pnpm pipeline` loads protected playground drafts under the same role, degrades reasoning bootstrap for actuator-only drafts without a WorkItem binding, and maps actuator dispatch outputs structurally: one-step pipeline envelopes keep their `.context` unwrap path (so `core:ptc` / `system:json_query` stay correct), while bare action payloads land on `last_capture` / `last_transform` / `export_as` (so `vcs:log` no longer fails empty-capture after a successful call). Full convergence onto payload-only dispatch results remains a follow-up at the SDK boundary. `--sessions` / `--emit-adf` report the scratch path; tenant-partition drafts print `pipeline:promote --tenant <slug>` and point humans at `pnpm kyberion approvals --approve` (not the tenant-reasoning `approve` verb). `actuator-to-pipeline-flow` (EN/JA) documents the tenant-trial operator notes: same-checkout scratch/journal, personal tenant profile, actuator-only degrade, capture channel, and promote identity.
