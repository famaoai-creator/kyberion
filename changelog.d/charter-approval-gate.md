- **Charter-aware approval gate** — `enforceApprovalGate` accepts `charter` (scope + the action's facts, supplied by the trusted caller). Inside an active accountability charter the action runs without a per-action approval and is audited against the responsible human; outside it a human decides (even where the legacy policy needed none); a standing tripwire blocks. Hardened policies (injection suspected, strict posture, dual-key, hard-coded shell/egress/secret/deploy) are never delegable. Callers that do not pass `charter` behave exactly as before.
- **Accountability report** — `approval_inbox charter [--send] [--hours N] [--locale en]`: what ran under each charter in force, what was held for a decision, budget use, standing tripwires and charters near expiry. Schedule it daily.
- **Charter at the decision-rights op** — `approval:evaluate_decision_rights` consults the tenant's active charter for `operational_spend` (as a payment) and `contract_signature`; other decision types are unchanged. A charter takes part even when the decision-rights matrix would not escalate (out-of-envelope spend needs a human). Spend is treated as irreversible with worst-case loss = the amount, so the charter must name `operational_spend` in `irreversible_named_actions`.
- **Decision-rights matrix wins by default** — when the matrix routes a decision to a human, a charter defers to it (and consumes no budget) unless the charter's `envelope.supersedes_decision_rights` is `true`. That flag is signed at acceptance and is rejected without an owner/officer authority basis with `evidence_ref`; a delegated charter cannot hold it unless its parent does.
- **Charter screen** — Settings › Organization and members › Responsibility charter. An owner enters spend limits, deputies, validity and two explicit choices (irreversible spend without asking; standing in for the decision-rights matrix), reviews the exact bilingual statement, agrees, and the charter takes effect. The accountable human is always the authenticated member; the signed text is bound by digest; amending replaces (and retires) the charter in force. The accountable human or a deputy can stop everything, resume, or retire the charter.
