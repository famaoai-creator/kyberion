category: Fixed
---

- daemon-watchdog: a first sighting (typically the first run after the host wakes) no longer emits a warning, a matching "recovered" record or a `daemon_restart` recovery request; it is reported as `pending` and only a confirmed outage (unhealthy on two consecutive runs) alerts and requests recovery. A recovery record is written only for an episode that alerted (`alerted` in the run state, kept across a sleep gap). Previously each wake produced several stale/recovered pairs and could queue a restart approval for the operator.
- runtime health trends: RSS growth is reported only when the post-GC heap floor (lower quartile of `heap_used_mb` in the first vs last third) also rose by ≥ 50 MB and the warning ratio, so a one-off RSS spike from a heavy in-process job or a GC saw-tooth no longer reads as a leak.
- operational learning: personal-tier machine signals are captured with the owner NHI the process claims (`KYBERION_NHI_ID`); without one they are skipped with a single daily "Operational learning paused" warning, instead of failing in the queue with `[MEMORY_SCOPE_REQUIRED] ... has no owner_nhi` and raising a "Learning candidate dropped" alert per signal.
