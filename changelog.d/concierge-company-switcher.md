- **Concierge company switcher narrows every screen** — the rail's switcher now lists only the companies (tenants) you may see, plus 個人 (personal) and, for viewers who may see every company, システム（未割当） (system: items that belong to no company). The choice is kept in the page URL (`?tenant=`) and the `kyberion_selected_tenant` cookie, and the server re-checks it on every request: 決める, the summary stream, hygiene, the memory queue, response status, work-inventory candidates and review digest, settings (members, invites, readiness, training, charters, config missions) and the conversation read only the selected company. A selection you are not allowed to see is ignored, never widened: a viewer scoped to one company always sees it, anyone else falls back to 個人; a viewer who may see every company is never pinned to one company. 個人 shows one count card per company, plus a システム card for all-company viewers (`GET /api/personal-summary`, with its own rate limit) instead of a mixed queue. If the tenant registry cannot be read, no company data is shown (warn log). The switcher stays visible even when the selected company has no profile. Each tab's summary stream follows its own URL. Response status now lists only the selected company's delegated tasks (attributed through their mission); host-wide queue counts are shown only to all-company viewers. A multi-company viewer now opens in 個人 instead of the server's write tenant. presence-studio, Chronos and the headless API keep their explicit `?tenant=` narrowing and do not read the switcher yet.
