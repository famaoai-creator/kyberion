- **Background-delegation notifications are stored in the tenant's own partition** — the completion notification queue (instruction and result excerpts) moved from the shared `active/shared/runtime/delegations/notifications.jsonl` into the delegation store partitions: `active/shared/runtime/delegation-records/system/notifications.jsonl` for untenanted delegations and `…/confidential/<tenant>/notifications.jsonl` for tenant ones, read and written only as the tenant-bound `delegation_record_store` role. A process bound to one tenant lists and claims only its own notifications and cannot enqueue one for another tenant; the unbound mission worker still drains every partition, and each notification is still delivered at most once. Tenant offboarding exports and removes the tenant's queue with its partition.
- **Upgrade**: nothing to run. The legacy shared queue is no longer appended to or rewritten at runtime. Its rows stay deliverable — to the tenant their `tenant_slug`, delegation record or mission belongs to, and to unbound processes — and a claimed legacy row is copied, claimed, into its partition so it is never delivered again; rows already claimed stay claimed. Tenant offboarding also exports, then prunes the tenant's rows from the legacy queue (audited as `SCOPE_OFFBOARD_DELEGATION_NOTIFICATION_PRUNE`). Rows that cannot be attributed remain and are visible to unbound processes only. `KYBERION_DELEGATION_NOTIFICATIONS_PATH` remains a test override that pins one queue file for every scope.
