- Graduated autonomy for resident dots (DL-10, `@agent/core/dot/dot-autonomy`): levels L0 shadow, L1 approve-all, L2 supervised (default, today's behaviour), L3 trusted (the learned decision floor lifts after one human approval and decays seven days after the rejection) and L4 autonomous (L3 plus notify → auto for policy `autonomy.relaxable_actions` that are reversible, touch no high-risk path, are not never_auto or a policy approve, while outcome success stays ≥ 0.8). Charter `autonomy.{initial_level,min_level,max_level}` bounds the level (default max L3); the charter floor and the policy gate are never lowered. State lives in `autonomy/<dot>.json`, and `autonomy-shadow.jsonl` records what the next level up would have decided against each human-settled decision. The supervisor step `dot-autonomy` demotes automatically (regressed outcomes, rejection streak, executor incidents; notify + audit; not below L1 unless `min_level` allows) and opens a promotion decision card (forced `approve`) that is applied only after a human approval settles in a later sweep. New `autonomy` status section and digest lines on level changes. `autonomous-ops-policy.json` gains an additive `autonomy` section (relaxable actions, promotion and demotion thresholds) with schema.
