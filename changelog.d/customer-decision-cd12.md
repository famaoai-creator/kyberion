- **Customer decision overview and learning (CD-12)** — `pnpm kyberion customer-decision overview --tenant <slug> [--case <id>] --json` prints a read-only overview of every case of the tenant (drafts included): per agenda the confirmed / provisional / awaiting-customer / awaiting-operator-confirmation / undecided counts, the respond-by date, the nearest override deadline, open change requests, pending hand-offs, due and upcoming reminders and held tasks, plus a tenant summary with the items that need attention sorted by urgency. It never reconciles, routes, notifies or writes, shows counts instead of the customer's free text, and refuses a `--tenant` other than the bound `KYBERION_TENANT` (JSON contract `CustomerDecisionOverview`). `customer-decision learning --tenant <slug> [--since <date>] [--dry-run]` counts how often our decisions and organization defaults were overturned, recurring questions and change requests, writes the tenant report `knowledge/confidential/<tenant>/decisions/_reports/learning-<date>.json` and queues improvement suggestions (change an organization default, revise options, clarify an entry; with minimum sample sizes) as organization learning candidates with the new source `customer_decision`, once per suggestion and date; accept one with `pnpm organization learning transition`. `learning --install --tenant <slug> --by user:<member>` installs the weekly tenant pipeline (`core:customer_decision_learning`).
- **Customer decisions card in the Chronos organization view (CD-12)** — the organization view of a selected tenant now shows a "Customer decisions" card: provisional items, items awaiting the customer or your confirmation, change requests, hand-offs and held tasks, a "needs attention" list ordered by urgency with days left or overdue, and per-case agenda-set rows with status counts, answer-by dates and override deadlines, plus the CLI commands to act (`customer-decision status`, `confirm`, `draft --from-tasks`). It is read through the new `GET /api/customer-decisions/overview`, which resolves exactly one tenant from the viewer (a `tenant` parameter only narrows; project-scoped viewers are refused) and runs the governed facade as a child bound to that tenant with no system role, so Chronos gains no access to the decision stores. Counts, names and dates only: no decision values, questions or customer free text.
