- **Customer decision overview and learning (CD-12, wave 1)** — `pnpm kyberion customer-decision overview --tenant <slug> [--case <id>] --json` prints a read-only overview of every case of the tenant (drafts included): per agenda the confirmed / provisional / awaiting-customer / awaiting-operator-confirmation / undecided counts, the respond-by date, the nearest override deadline, open change requests, pending hand-offs, due and upcoming reminders and held tasks, plus a tenant summary with the items that need attention sorted by urgency. It never reconciles, routes, notifies or writes, shows counts instead of the customer's free text, and refuses a `--tenant` other than the bound `KYBERION_TENANT` (the JSON contract `CustomerDecisionOverview` is what the Chronos organization view will read). `customer-decision learning --tenant <slug> [--since <date>] [--dry-run]` counts how often our decisions and organization defaults were overturned, recurring questions and change requests, writes the tenant report `knowledge/confidential/<tenant>/decisions/_reports/learning-<date>.json` and queues improvement suggestions (change an organization default, revise options, clarify an entry; with minimum sample sizes) as organization learning candidates with the new source `customer_decision`, once per suggestion and date; accept one with `pnpm organization learning transition`. `learning --install --tenant <slug> --by user:<member>` installs the weekly tenant pipeline (`core:customer_decision_learning`).
