category: Added
---

- **Governed dot proposals**: a dot no longer acts on its own during a wake. It submits proposals, through the `dot_propose_action` tool or a fenced `dot-proposals` block. Each proposal is checked against the charter's `allowed_work_shapes`, `max_concurrent_delegations` and handoff targets, then passes the autonomous-ops gate. The runtime derives the policy action, `dot_delegate_work` or `dot_handoff`; the dot never chooses it. Both actions score notify and have a 60-minute veto window, so they arrive as veto cards. The charter's `decisions.default_decision` sets a floor the policy can raise but never lower, and the veto window is the larger of the charter's and the policy's. Approved work becomes a WorkItem carrying the dot's identity (`dot:<id>`) and authority role, with an audit entry for each one.
- **Charter notification is enforced**: decision cards and notices go to the charter's `deliver_to`, respect `quiet_hours` (urgent events still go out) and are summarized in a `digest` sent at most once per cron minute. Message content stays in the local inbox unless the charter sets `notification.delivery_mode: "live"`. Devices subscribed to Web Push still get a content-free wake-up. A veto card that reaches only the inbox waits for a human decision. Waiting decisions count toward `max_concurrent_delegations`.
- **Parked actions settle on their own**: the supervisor ticks autonomy veto windows on every sweep. It runs approved actions after re-checking the charter scope, and declines rejected or expired ones; a declined proposal is not asked again for 24 hours. Settlement can be safely retried after a crash.
- **Dot teams**: a charter can declare `team.responsibilities` (activation refuses a second active dot with the same responsibility), `team.accepts_handoffs_from` (a handoff wakes the target dot) and `team.goal_ref`.
- **Learning loop**: `goal.signal_probes` measure success signals on a schedule. Each human approve or reject is recorded as feedback. A rejection raises the dot's floor to `approve` until three approvals decided by a human follow, or until the rejection is 30 days old. Veto-window silence and approvals by agents or services do not count. A rejection also creates a distill candidate. `dot status` shows actions, items waiting on the operator, signals and recent feedback.
- **Test isolation**: under vitest the approval store is now separate for each worker (`vitest-approvals/pool-<id>/`), so parallel test files that clear a channel no longer race each other.
