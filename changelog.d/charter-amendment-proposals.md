- **Charter change proposals** — the ledger now records the limit in force and the value a denied action needed. The accountability report lists `amendment_proposals` per charter field (smallest widening that admits every denial in the window). The charter pane shows today's/this month's spend as bars and the proposals, and "put this in the form" pre-fills a money/loss limit (raising parent limits with it). Nothing is applied automatically: the owner still reviews the signed statement and accepts a new charter.
