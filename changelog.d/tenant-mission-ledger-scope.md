- **Tenant mission ledger events stay in the tenant's scope** — `ledger.record` now takes a record's scope from its mission (the mission state's tier, tenant, organization and project, then the mission's location) when the record omits them, so a confidential tenant mission's `MISSION_CREATE` / `MISSION_ACTIVATE` / `MISSION_DISTILL` / `MISSION_FINISH` and orchestration events are no longer recorded with tier `public` or scope `system`. The metadata entry of a tenant mission (and the detailed entry of a tenant record without a mission ledger) is written to that tenant's own hash-chained partition, `active/shared/runtime/tenant-ledger/<tier>/<tenant>/ledger.jsonl`, as the new tenant-bound `tenant_ledger_store` role; the system ledger (`active/audit/system-ledger.jsonl`) no longer receives any tenant slug or tenant mission metadata. Untenanted (public / shared) missions keep today's behavior. Existing system-ledger entries are not rewritten. `pnpm audit:verify`, `system:pulse_status` and mission `finish` verify the tenant partitions too (one chain per file; another tenant's partition is reported as withheld to a tenant-bound process), `loadForScope` reaches the partitions of the tenants a filter names under the reader's own tier-guard decision, and tenant offboarding exports and removes the tenant's partitions. If you read tenant mission events from the system ledger, read the tenant partition instead.
