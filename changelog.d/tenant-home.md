- **Tenant company assets moved into the tenant home `knowledge/confidential/<tenant>/`** — the company descriptor (`customer.json` is now `company.json`), organization profile, vision, org chart, OKRs, decision rights, finance, brand, design and channel bindings are tenant data, so they now live inside the tenant's confidentiality boundary (tenant scope protects them) instead of the top-level `customer/<tenant>/`. Resolvers read only the tenant home (the `customer` `source_kind` is gone: `confidential` / `tenant`), and the organization profile follows the active tenant (`KYBERION_TENANT`), not the stance. `CompanyAggregate.customer_ref` is now `company_ref`; `identity_ref` is removed (the operator's identity belongs to the stance). The tenant audit mirror moved to `active/shared/runtime/audit-mirror/confidential/<tenant>/`, a tenant store partition only `audit_mirror_writer` may write. Channel bindings are read as the tenant-bound `customer_channel_binding_store`, and the bind conflict check fails closed when another tenant's bindings cannot be read. Customer records (`deals/`) stay in `customer/<tenant>/` until S3. Migration required: run `pnpm migration --dry-run`, then `pnpm migration` (`0002-tenant-home`, after `0001-stance-home`).
