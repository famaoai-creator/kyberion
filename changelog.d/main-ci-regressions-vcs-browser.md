- The shared git verb surface now registers its `local-git` backend itself, so callers that never imported `local-git-backend` (the vcs-actuator, mission development workspaces) no longer fail with `[VCS_BACKEND] no backend registered`. Crystallized browser recordings validate again under the authored op contracts: `fill`/`press`/`wait` accept the recorded-target fields (`role`, `name`, `dom_path`, `high_risk`) that `click` already accepted, `fill` accepts `classification`/`secret_ref`, and `click` accepts the recorded `selection`. The draft-review markers (`original_op`, `needs_selector`) are left out of contract validation.
