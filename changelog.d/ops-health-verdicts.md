- Add `libs/core/ops-health.ts` (`evaluateOpsHealth`): per-item resident-ops findings — overdue / failing schedules, chronos crash loop and failing ticks, an empty registry while pipelines declare schedules, stale or missing backup archives, a failed `backup-daily`, a missing / stale / failed / skipped restore drill, a stale or failed janitor, missing required units, units pointing at another checkout, legacy units, units double-owned with an enabled surface, a chronos tick in progress for longer than 8h, and a trigger delivery store past its growth warning level (`trigger-store-growth`, warn only). Each finding names a concrete next command.
- Baseline: L10 now fails on per-schedule overdue / failing and crash-loop findings (a fresh heartbeat from a crash-looping chronos no longer passes), and the new L12 resident-ops layer reports the rest; both degrade only to `needs_attention`. The report gains an `ops_health` block. `pnpm kyberion doctor` prints the same findings as verdict lines.
- Chronos heartbeats carry `started_at`, `restarts_1h` / `restarts_24h` and the last tick outcome; `last_tick_ok_at` moves only after a tick completes, while a long tick (for example a multi-hour backup) refreshes the heartbeat every minute as `tick_in_progress` so baseline and the watchdog keep it alive. A tick in progress longer than 8h counts as unhealthy.
- Trigger delivery journal retention is age-based: finished receipts stay for the full 7-day dedupe window (stable keys never re-deliver early), byte pressure trims the oldest cron receipts first, and pending claims whose lease expired over 7 days ago are dropped.
- Backups leave out only the `tmp` / `staging` / `cache` floors and retention catalog entries marked `"backup": "exclude"`; cache/tmp-classed staging stores (distill and promotion candidates, queued orchestration events, the presence bridge runtime) are archived again. The re-fetchable `active/shared/data-vault` cache and the soft-delete floor `active/archive/.trash` are excluded.
- When stable keys alone fill the trigger delivery journal, the inline rotation backs off (re-parse only after 256 KB of growth or an hour) and warns at most once an hour. Add `pnpm kyberion scheduler trigger-store rotate`, an approval-gated way to shed in-window stable keys from a journal that keeps growing: dry-run by default, `--request-approval` then `--apply --approval-request-id <id>` moves the dropped records to a segment, keeps in-flight claims, the newest N stable keys and recent cron receipts, and records the rotation on the audit chain. The `trigger-store-growth` finding names it once the journal passes its warning level; the journal has no append cap.
- The restore drill writes `active/shared/runtime/state/backup-drill-last.json` on success, failure and a distinct `no_archive` skip; the baseline-submitted janitor run keeps its output in `active/shared/logs/process/baseline-janitor-maintenance.log` and records `janitor-last-failure.json` on a non-zero exit.
