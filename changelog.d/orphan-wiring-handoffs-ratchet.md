- **Orphan hand-offs wired, orphan ratchet added (OW-07)** — scheduled gate callers can route an operator-bound verdict through the HITL decision loop (`KYBERION_AUTONOMY_DECISION_ROUTING=1`: the daemon watchdog raises one restart decision card per daemon, auto-checkpoint parks a digest card; `routeAutonomousDecision` gains `dedupeKey`). `pnpm kyberion deals` can now build a price-book quote, draft a contract, record a contract review and hand a won deal to its SDLC mission. The error-category, browser-failure and knowledge-relevance judgment assists are wired at their call sites but ask no provider until a calibration fit exists (`KYBERION_JUDGMENT_ASSISTS=off` disables them). Governance data left by retired loaders and the unused write side of the mesh topic registry moved to `retired/`. A new `orphans` PR gate (`scripts/check_orphans.ts`, baseline `knowledge/product/governance/orphan-baseline.json`) fails when a new unimported core module, uninvoked script, unreachable pipeline or unreferenced actuator op appears; the 40 commands registered in the previous wave now carry help descriptions and groups.
