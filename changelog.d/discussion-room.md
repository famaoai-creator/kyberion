- **Discussion Room** — Chronos gains a facilitated multi-agent discussion surface at `/discussion`. Give it a goal and Kyberion staffs a team from the organization's agent pool (facilitator, researcher, devil's advocate, scribe plus goal-driven seats), then runs a turn-taking discussion toward a decision. Three panes show the team and agenda, the live agent conversation, and the situation (consensus gauge, stance map, open issues, votes, decision); a command center lets a `localadmin` operator pause, inject a message, ask a specific agent, redirect the agenda, call a vote, or conclude. Visibility follows the viewer's tenant / organization / project scope. Speakers use the registered reasoning backend when available and fall back to a deterministic scripted engine for offline demos. Decisions flow into Kyberion: the minutes are registered as a deliverable (Deliverables inbox) and each next step is offered as a WorkItem proposal that becomes a backlog WorkItem, with the room's tenant / organization / project / mission context, only after a human approves. A room can be linked to a mission you can already see, and work items link back to the discussion they came from. The Discussion Room is also part of the Chronos navigation under Decisions.
