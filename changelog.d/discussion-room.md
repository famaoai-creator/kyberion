- **Discussion Room** — Chronos gains a facilitated multi-agent discussion surface at `/discussion`. Give it a goal and Kyberion staffs a team from the organization's agent pool (facilitator, researcher, devil's advocate, scribe plus goal-driven seats), then runs a turn-taking discussion toward a decision. Three panes show the team and agenda, the live agent conversation, and the situation (consensus gauge, stance map, open issues, votes, decision); a command center lets a `localadmin` operator pause, inject a message, ask a specific agent, redirect the agenda, call a vote, or conclude. Visibility follows the viewer's tenant / organization / project scope. Speakers use the registered reasoning backend when available and fall back to a deterministic scripted engine for offline demos.
