- **Discussion Room** — Chronos gains a facilitated multi-agent discussion surface at `/discussion`. Give it a goal and Kyberion staffs a team from the organization's agent pool (facilitator, researcher, devil's advocate, scribe plus goal-driven seats), then runs a turn-taking discussion toward a decision. Three panes show the team and agenda, the live agent conversation, and the situation (consensus gauge, stance map, open issues, votes, decision); a command center lets a `localadmin` operator pause, inject a message, ask a specific agent, redirect the agenda, call a vote, or conclude. Visibility follows the viewer's tenant / organization / project scope. Speakers use the registered reasoning backend when available and fall back to a deterministic scripted engine for offline demos. Decisions flow into Kyberion: the minutes are registered as a deliverable (Deliverables inbox) and each next step is offered as a WorkItem proposal that becomes a backlog WorkItem, with the room's tenant / organization / project / mission context, only after a human approves. A room can be linked to a mission you can already see, and work items link back to the discussion they came from. The Discussion Room is also part of the Chronos navigation under Decisions. The minutes are now also an interactive decision brief (HTML deliverable): consensus trend, stance map, objections and how they resolved, and editable next actions. Reviewing it (accept / send back / reject) creates the kept WorkItems, reopens the room with your comment, or records the rejection; accepting can also raise a mission-start approval request, and an approved request lets you start the mission through mission_controller, with the mission id threaded back onto the room, WorkItems and deliverables.
