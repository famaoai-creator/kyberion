- **Execution metrics partitioned by tier and tenant** — `MetricsCollector.record` no longer appends rows whose scope carries a tenant or a personal/confidential tier to the repo-wide `work/metrics/execution-metrics.jsonl`; they land in `active/shared/runtime/execution-metrics/<tier>/<tenant|shared>/execution-metrics.jsonl`, the same layout and mechanism as the resource-usage ledger (one shared `PartitionedMetricsLedger`). `loadHistory({ read })` takes the same read scopes as `loadResourceUsageHistory`, and every reader now names one: mission retrospective (its mission's partition plus system), cost report and Chronos cost summary (the filtered tenants plus system, else the aggregate), org budget governor (its tenant plus system, else the aggregate), spend guard, operator home, terminal HUD, `pnpm doctor`, health-degradation watch and soak regressions (`{ all: true }`); learning signals stay system-only. `reportFromHistory` / `detectRegressions` accept a read scope and order merged rows by timestamp. Pre-partition rows stay readable as legacy, gated by their own scope. Tenant offboarding exports and soft-deletes the tenant's execution-metrics partitions and exports, then prunes, its legacy rows (approval-gated, audited as `SCOPE_OFFBOARD_EXECUTION_METRICS_PRUNE`). The new root has a retention-catalog entry, is a `tenant_scope.protected_prefixes` root and is sandboxed under Vitest.
- **Persona read gate for the metrics ledgers** — personal/confidential partitions of the usage ledger and the execution-metrics ledger now follow tier-guard's persona rules for `knowledge/personal/` and `knowledge/confidential/` (the same grant evaluation, reused; no new persona table). A persona that may not read the knowledge tier no longer reads those partitions or their legacy rows; aggregate readers skip them with a diagnostic debug line instead of failing. Processes without such a grant (for example a `worker` without the `mission_controller` role) therefore see only system and permitted-tier rows, including in spend and budget checks.
