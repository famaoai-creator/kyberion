category: Changed
---

- Concierge is now the single workspace shell. New pages show how everything connects:
  - `/w`: overview with what needs attention, the company → organization → project tree, and recent missions.
  - `/w/<kind>`: a searchable, filterable list for each kind of entity.
  - `/w/<kind>/<id>`: one entity with its lineage breadcrumb, the things it contains, and the people and agents involved.
- The shared rail is regrouped into Work (Inbox, Ask, Missions, Work items, Approvals), Organization (Overview, Organizations, Projects, People & agents), Resources and Manage. The duplicated presence-studio Home, Progress and legacy `/work` entries are no longer on the rail.
- ⌘K also searches entities.
