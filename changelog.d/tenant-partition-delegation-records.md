- **Delegation traces and records are stored in the tenant's own partition** — the delegated-task trace and the resumable delegation records (instruction, context and result text) moved from the shared `active/shared/observability/delegations.jsonl` / `active/shared/observability/delegations/` to `active/shared/runtime/delegation-records/`: untenanted delegations under `system/`, tenant delegations under `confidential/<tenant>/` (`delegations.jsonl`, `records/<id>.json`, `records/child-inbox/`). Tenant partitions are read and written only as the tenant-bound `delegation_record_store` role, so a process bound to one tenant cannot load, list or resume another tenant's delegations; a cold-resume worker runs bound to its delegation's tenant. Tenant offboarding exports and removes the tenant's partition (target kind `tenant_delegations`). `chronos_gateway` now writes `active/shared/runtime/delegation-records/system/` instead of the legacy trace file.
- **Legacy resident-dot copies are removed with their tenant** — tenant offboarding now also exports and soft-deletes the tenant's pre-partition dot state under `active/shared/runtime/dot/tenants/<slug>/` (audited like the other tenant runtime state). For a tenant that stays active, moving that copy still needs the operator-reviewed `pnpm kyberion dot migrate-state`. The tenant's partitioned dot state (`dot/<tier>/<tenant>/`) is unchanged: it stays review-required and is not removed automatically.
- **Upgrade**: nothing to run. The legacy shared files are no longer appended to. A legacy record stays readable — by the tenant its mission (or `tenant_slug`) belongs to, and by unbound operator processes — and moves into its partition on its next write. Tenant offboarding also exports, then removes the tenant's legacy records and child inboxes and prunes its rows from the legacy trace and its rotated segments (audited as `SCOPE_OFFBOARD_DELEGATION_TRACE_PRUNE`). Legacy rows with neither a tenant stamp nor a mission cannot be attributed and remain; they are visible to unbound processes only.
