- **Tenant resident Dots can reason again, through a real task credential binding**: a tenant Dot woken by the agent-runtime supervisor failed every wake with `[CREDENTIAL_BINDING_REQUIRED]` because it had no claim and no mission/task lineage. Each tenant Dot now has one standing Issue on a deterministic `MSN-DOT-*` lineage; every wake and every executor item is one claim on a reusable child step (one per kind, so a Dot owns at most three WorkItems) held by the Dot's canonical NHI (lease renewed by the supervisor; a step left behind by a crash is recovered by the next run, never escalated as uncertain dot work). Only charters loaded from `knowledge/confidential/<tenant>/dots/` bind that tenant's credentials, and the binding is minted from that claim through the existing claimed-task dispatch. Credentials still come only from the tenant owner's `credential.use` grant, re-validated at every use; tenantless Dots are unchanged. A missing or expired grant fails the wake with `[DOT_CREDENTIAL_GRANT_MISSING]` / `[DOT_CREDENTIAL_GRANT_EXPIRED]` and the exact command to fix it, counted by the wake circuit (`reason_code: credential_grant`).
- **Standing grants can be extended as the same grant**: `pnpm kyberion reasoning credentials extend --tenant <t> --grant <id> [--days N]` opens an owner approval bound to the grant id, current expiry and length; `--approval <id>` applies it. Only `expires_at` moves (at most the capability maximum from approval), the step is audited (`grant.extended`) and re-verified at every use. Revoked grants are never extended; expired ones only within 7 days. `allocations` now shows `days_left`, and the supervisor sends a once-a-day ops alert from 7 days before expiry.
