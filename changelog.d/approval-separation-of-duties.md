- **Approval separation of duties (opt-in)** — `separation_of_duties.enabled` in `approval-policy.json` (default `false`, also settable in a customer overlay; global, not per tenant or organization). When on:
  - Every approval decided through the approval store (`decideApprovalRequest`) is refused with `[POLICY_VIOLATION] Separation of duties` and audited when the decider's recorded identity equals a recorded requester identity, when the request has no requester, when the decider is empty or a surface placeholder (`concierge`, `chronos-localadmin`, `sovereign-user`, …), or when the surface took the decider as free text from its caller (MCP `kyberion.approval.decide`, approval-actuator `decide`).
  - Consumers that turn an approved record into an effect re-check it (`assertApprovalUsable`), so a self-approval recorded while the setting was off no longer takes effect after it is switched on. The approval gate and the provider-attestation `--request-approval` open a fresh request instead of reusing such a record.
  - The check compares recorded identity strings after normalisation (case, NFKC, `user:`/`agent:`-style prefixes). It cannot tell that two different strings are the same person; in particular, the default CLI flow records the persona as requester and the onboarding name as approver. Approval flows outside the approval store (e.g. `service_recording review --approve`) are not covered.
  - Agent-requested, human-approved requests keep working. An unreadable `approval-policy.json` now blocks approving decisions and the use of approvals with a diagnostic, even while the setting is off (plugin activation degrades to pending instead). The mission brief approval page now records the operator identity resolved server-side instead of a name typed on the page.
- **Pasteable attestation apply command** — `pnpm onboarding llm attest … --request-approval` and `pnpm tenant attest-provider … --request-approval` now print the follow-up apply command for POSIX shells with every bound value (plan, basis, attested-by, valid-for-days) quoted instead of `...`. Values containing a backslash or control characters are rejected at parse time. `pnpm tenant attest-provider … --request-approval` without `--apply` now opens the request instead of printing a dry-run.
