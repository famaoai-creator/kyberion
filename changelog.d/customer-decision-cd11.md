- **Customer decision reminders and hand-offs (CD-11, wave 1)** — agendas take a respond-by date (`customer-decision draft --respond-by <YYYY-MM-DD>`, part of the content hash only when set). `pnpm kyberion customer-decision remind --tenant <slug> [--case <id>] [--dry-run]` sends the reminders due today (UTC): the day before and on the respond-by date to deciders with a valid invite who have not answered, and two days before a date override deadline while our proposal is still in force. Each goes to the decider's bound channel (`actor-add --channel <surface>:<channel_id>`, checked against the tenant's channel bindings) through `sendToCustomer`; the fixed reminder text counts as routine business only when the body is exactly the registered template, so a tenant charter that delegates customer outbound sends it unattended and everything else waits for approval. Deciders without a channel become operator inbox tasks (ids only). One record per agenda × decider × kind × date keeps a second run from sending twice. `customer-decision reminders --install --tenant <slug>` places the daily tenant pipeline (`core:customer_decision_reminders`; Chronos runs it after a project-trust approval). Answers outside the options, comments and a new agenda-level `general_question` in `respond` are handed to the operator after each response (inbox entry and notification, ids and counts only), retried by `status`, which also lists upcoming, sent and held reminders.
