- **SCIM 2.0 Users provisioning** — an organization's IdP (Microsoft Entra ID, Okta, …) can now create, update and deactivate its members through concierge `/scim/v2` (`Users`, `Users/{id}`, `ServiceProviderConfig`, `ResourceTypes`, `Schemas`; `filter=userName eq` / `externalId eq`, `startIndex`/`count` paging, PatchOp, `application/scim+json`, SCIM error envelope, rate limited). The IdP only anchors identity: members land in the same member registry as invites, new members get the token's default role (viewer unless the owner chose operator/approver — never owner), SCIM never changes roles, DELETE / `active:false` suspend (never delete), and a member shared with another organization keeps its global attributes. SCIM changes only members below owner whose role ranks at most the token's default role, reactivates only members it suspended (`suspended_by`), and sets or moves `externalId` only on members whose every identity this organization's SCIM bound (`provisioned_by`) or that have no identity and no access token; a PUT without `externalId` leaves the binding alone. `externalId` binds the member's external identity under the OIDC issuer fixed on the token, stored exactly as configured (sign-in matches `iss` verbatim) and compared through one normaliser. Provisioning tokens are per tenant, issued and revoked by an owner with `pnpm organization scim-token issue|list|revoke` (chat-surface issuers such as `https://slack.com` are refused), stored as a sha256 hash only, refused while the tenant is suspended or unregistered, rejected by every other surface API and audited on issue, use and revoke. `meta.location` follows the declared public origin (`KYBERION_OIDC_PUBLIC_BASE_URL(S)`). An owner re-linking a SCIM-made identity takes it over. See [SCIM_PROVISIONING_OPERATIONS](docs/developer/SCIM_PROVISIONING_OPERATIONS.ja.md).
