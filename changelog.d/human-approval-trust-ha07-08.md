category: Security

- **Approve with a passkey (A3)** — members register passkeys under Concierge Settings › Profile › Passkeys. Approval cards that require A3 (dual-key secret operations, secret grants, vault direct writes, authority grants, policy changes and project trust) now show "Approve with passkey". The server issues a single-use, 120-second challenge bound to the request id, the decision, the presented digest and the expiry. It verifies the rpID and origin (taken from the governed public origin `KYBERION_OIDC_PUBLIC_BASE_URLS` / `KYBERION_OIDC_PUBLIC_BASE_URL`), the challenge, that it is unused and unexpired, that the credential belongs to the deciding member, and that the signature counter advanced. Only then is the decision recorded as `passkey`. The approval store refuses a `passkey` decision that does not consume a verified challenge. Only public keys are stored, in `knowledge/personal/members/{id}/passkeys.json`. The card sends the digest it showed when it asks for the challenge, and a request that changed since then is refused. Later stages of a staged workflow can also be signed with a passkey.
- **Assurance mode is governed policy** — `approval-policy.json` now carries `assurance_mode` (`warn` by default) and per-rule `min_assurance`. `KYBERION_APPROVAL_ASSURANCE=enforce` can still tighten it, but it can no longer relax a policy that says `enforce`. An unreadable policy is treated as `enforce`. In warn mode, shortfalls are still recorded as `assurance_shortfall` in the audit and notified to the operator.
- **Migration** — pending human-only requests are judged by the current rules when they are decided. Older records without `min_assurance` count as A2, and pending project-trust requests are raised to A3. Decisions already recorded are not re-graded.
- **Passkey enrollment is protected** — passkeys can be added or removed only from a member's own signed-in browser or OIDC session. Registry bearer tokens, unauthenticated local viewers and agent principals are refused. Once a member has a usable passkey, adding or removing one first needs a confirmation with that passkey (a single-use, 120-second step-up bound to the purpose, the member and the passkey concerned). Confirming returns a single-use token to that browser only, and the change must present it, so another session of the same member cannot use the confirmation. A passkey added without that confirmation cannot approve A3 requests until its cooldown ends (`passkey_enrollment_cooldown_hours` in `approval-policy.json`, default 24). The refusal and the settings pane say when it becomes usable. The cooldown is at least one hour. Every add and remove is written to the audit chain and notified to the operator, saying whether it was confirmed with an existing passkey, when a new passkey becomes usable, and whether a removed passkey was still cooling down.
- **Pending requests created before this release** are also judged against today's policy rule (found by recorded rule id, or by the gate's intent id for older records): a pending dual-key or policy-change request now needs A3. Expired passkey challenges are removed by the daily `approval-store-hygiene` pipeline.
