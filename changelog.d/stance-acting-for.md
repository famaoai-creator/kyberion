- **Stance is now the acting-for selection; `KYBERION_CUSTOMER` is retired** (Concept Model v2 D4, plan S2) — a stance says which permitted tenant the operator is acting for, as which Organization and Org Role, declared as `acting_for` (`tenant_slug`, optional `organization_id`, `org_role`) in `knowledge/personal/stances/<slug>/stance.json`. `pnpm stance:switch` rejects a tenant that is not an active profile in `knowledge/personal/tenants/` (`[STANCE_NOT_PERMITTED]`) and writes `KYBERION_TENANT` / `KYBERION_ORGANIZATION_ID` into `stance.env` beside `KYBERION_STANCE`, so switching stance switches the data scope; a stance without `acting_for` keeps the old behaviour (overlay only). `@agent/core/stance-resolver` adds `resolveStance`, `narrowViewerScopeToStance` (narrows a `ViewerContext` and never widens it) and `stanceResourceBinding` (binds a `ResourceAccessScope` ceiling). `KYBERION_CUSTOMER` is read for one more release only when `KYBERION_STANCE` is unset and `knowledge/personal/stances/<slug>/` exists; otherwise it is ignored — both with a deprecation warning. tier-guard now reads it through the same path, and a malformed stance slug expands `${KYBERION_STANCE}` policy paths to `NONE`. Customer tone and contact stay in the tenant's channel bindings and customer records. Migration: replace `KYBERION_CUSTOMER` with `KYBERION_STANCE` in shells and service units, and add `acting_for` to stances that act for a tenant.
