- **PPTX text fit is measured for every protocol, before and after rendering.** `pptx_layout_preflight` and `pptx_render` used to read only the fit results that the brief and HTML slide builders record, so a hand-authored or template-derived protocol always passed. Labels then wrapped inside chevrons and body text ran past the box in the rendered deck. Both ops now measure every text box from its elements. The width model sets full-width glyphs, punctuation and spaces at 1em, adds the renderer's ¼em space between Japanese and Latin text, and widens Latin for BIZ UD fonts. It also applies the bodyPr insets (0.1in / 0.05in by default) and the text rectangle of preset shapes such as chevron, homePlate, roundRect and ellipse, and uses a line height of size × 1.2 × lineSpacing. Issues are reported as `overflow` (text leaves the box) or `unexpected_wrap` (a label, a centred line or a box sized to its authored lines wraps a paragraph that has no break). `params.text_fit` = `never` (default; log and export as `textFit`) | `error` | `warn` makes a step fail on them. The new capture op **`media:pptx_render_check`** converts the rendered deck to PDF with LibreOffice, reads where each line landed and reports the same two kinds with the slide, element and a text excerpt. `fail_on` defaults to `error`. Without LibreOffice the op returns `status: unavailable` with the model's findings instead of failing. Against LibreOffice 25.8, the model and the render agreed on 1,114 of 1,200 threshold-sweep labels; the other 86 were sized exactly to their text, and the model counts those as wrapped. On the repository's PPTX examples and fragments, every issue the model raised before rendering was also found in the LibreOffice render.
