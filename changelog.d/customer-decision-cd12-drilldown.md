- **Customer decision drill-down (CD-12)** — `pnpm kyberion customer-decision overview --tenant <slug> --case <id> --detail --json` prints one case in full for operators (JSON contract `CustomerDecisionCaseDetail`): per agenda set the entries with their status, decision layer, override deadline, whether you confirmed them and the question cut to 200 characters, plus change requests, hand-offs, reminders (records and upcoming), invite counts, Bolt-review routing and the mission tasks that wait on the case. `overview --tenant <slug> --learning [--date YYYY-MM-DD] --json` prints the learning report as operators triage it (`CustomerDecisionLearningView`). Both are read-only and never carry a decision value, our decision or the organization default value, a rationale, background or option, who decided, comments, general-question text or participant names; a default change only shows as "suggested". A missing case or report exits with `[CUSTOMER_DECISION_CASE_NOT_FOUND]` / `[CUSTOMER_DECISION_REPORT_NOT_FOUND]`.
- **Case, agenda-set and learning views in Chronos (CD-12)** — the "Customer decisions" card's attention and case rows now open the case and its agenda sets, and "Open learning" opens the learning report; the view is kept in the address bar (`cd_view`, `cd_case`, `cd_agenda`, `cd_date`, cleared when the tenant changes), with a breadcrumb, focus moved to the opened view and back to the row on return, held tasks linking to the mission, and the CLI commands filled with the real ids. They read the new `GET /api/customer-decisions/case` and `GET /api/customer-decisions/learning`, which share the overview's viewer scope (one tenant from the viewer, project-scoped viewers refused, confidential tier and the tenant's organization checked), validate the case id and date before any child runs, and map not found / not built / timeout to 404 / 503 / 504.
