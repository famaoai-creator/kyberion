# Tenant company starter files

Company assets of a tenant: descriptor (`customer.json`), vision, brand and
channel bindings. They are tenant data (custody `tenant`), not part of a stance.

These files still materialize into the legacy `customer/{tenant}/` directory
until S2 of the store-catalog guard design moves company assets to
`knowledge/confidential/{tenant}/`
([design](../../docs/developer/improvement-plans-2026-11/STORE_CATALOG_GRANT_GUARD_DESIGN_2026-11-05.ja.md)).
