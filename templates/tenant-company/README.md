# Tenant company starter files

Company assets of a tenant: descriptor (`company.json`), vision, brand and
channel bindings. They are tenant data (custody `tenant`), not part of a stance,
and live in the tenant home `knowledge/confidential/{tenant}/`
(`libs/core/organization/tenant-home.ts`), inside the tenant's confidentiality
boundary. See the
[store-catalog guard design](../../docs/developer/improvement-plans-2026-11/STORE_CATALOG_GRANT_GUARD_DESIGN_2026-11-05.ja.md).
