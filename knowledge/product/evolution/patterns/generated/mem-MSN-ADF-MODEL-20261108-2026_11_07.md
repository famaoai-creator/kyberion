# Verify small orchestration kernels with independent finite safety models

Combine exhaustive finite-state safety checking with production trace conformance and explicit assurance limits.

## Applicability

- mission
- mission:MSN-ADF-MODEL-20261108

## Reusable Steps

1. Combine exhaustive finite-state safety checking with production trace conformance and explicit assurance limits

## Expected Outcome

Apply this pattern to small deterministic orchestration kernels whose transition graph is finite. Specify transitions independently of production and enumerate reachable states against named safety invariants. Compare concrete production traces, handoff identities and failure propagation against the specification. Treat exploration-budget exhaustion as inconclusive, preserve shortest counterexamples, and include a deliberately invalid model to verify that the checker discriminates. State keys must encode every fact affecting successors and invariants; model states must remain immutable and successor functions deterministic. Document the explored graph, outcome combinations and excluded behavior. This establishes safety of the supplied abstraction plus tested implementation conformance, not a theorem-prover proof, arbitrary concurrency coverage or liveness guarantee.
