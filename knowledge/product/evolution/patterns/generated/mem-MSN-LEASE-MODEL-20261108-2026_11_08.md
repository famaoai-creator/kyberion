# Preserve lease backend exclusion across synchronous and asynchronous callbacks

Share namespace admission and separate callback results from success-settling queue tails.

## Applicability

- mission
- mission:MSN-LEASE-MODEL-20261108

## Reusable Steps

1. Share namespace admission and separate callback results from success-settling queue tails

## Expected Outcome

A backend mutex must span the full protected callback independently of lease TTL. Sync and async paths share admission; reject sync while async ownership is queued or active. Keep the public callback result or original error separate from a private always-settling queue tail. Cleanup compares the exact stored tail identity so an older completion cannot remove a successor. Preserve FIFO and acquisition delay after predecessor failure. Validate with deferred callbacks, injected or fake clocks, red-before-green regressions, and a bounded independent model with a negative control. Synchronous callbacks must return synchronously; awaiting reentrant acquisition of the same namespace is unsupported. The assurance covers one-runtime memory exclusion and KV-stub queue semantics, not distributed consensus or crash recovery.
