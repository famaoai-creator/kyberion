# Actuator discovery must be descriptive and fail closed

Keep discovery separate from execution and provide exact operation contracts alongside compact listings.

## Applicability

- mission:MSN-ACTUATOR-UX-20261010

## Expected Outcome

Actuator list, describe, and check discovery operations must never invoke action handlers. Compact operation listings support browsing; authored schemas and examples provide exact parameter requirements for execution. Missing or invalid discovery metadata must fail closed with an actionable diagnostic rather than invoking a handler or presenting an unreliable contract.
