# Initialize command capabilities at their authority boundary

Separate provider-free command handling, host preparation, and task-bound provider construction to avoid call-order-dependent credential failures.

## Hint Scope

mission

## Trigger Phrases

- Help, lifecycle bookkeeping, and native capture must not initialize reasoning providers. Prepare only resolver and factory registration on the host; construct private task reasoning chains after the canonical claim and credential binding. Optional provider discovery may tolerate only CREDENTIAL_BINDING_REQUIRED; explicit secret resolution and scope or owner mismatches remain strict. Invalidate scope caches when persisted scope changes, and keep temporary environment-only scope reads out of the shared cache. Supervisor readiness checks should defer provider initialization until binding exists while preserving recovery for genuine provider failures. Verification included independent review, focused leak tests, and command probes under an unbound tenant.
