# Record review deliverables before re-running review gates

A review can be recorded as approved yet the task gate stays blocked when the required REVIEW-<task>.md deliverable is missing; reviewer independence is only evaluated once implementer evidence and the deliverable exist.

## Hint Scope

mission

## Trigger Phrases

- During MSN-ONBOARDING-UX-20261022 the ux-review approval was recorded twice but remained blocked until evidence/ux-contract.json and REVIEW-contract_authoring-ux-contract.md existed and the implementer actor was recorded. Lesson: write the task deliverable and implementer evidence first, then record the review.
