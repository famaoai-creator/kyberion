# Bind scoped capture authority through the actual transport

Metadata authorization alone does not prove which credentials or authority will be consumed.

## Hint Scope

mission

## Trigger Phrases

- When a scoped collector is added, bind the selected connection and named credential references through the engine boundary. Revalidate catalog ownership, subject authorization and binding policy at issuance, consumption and immediately before transport. Never substitute preset kind/risk metadata for the canonical registry. Verify stronger registry requirements reject before broker use or transport; keep exact viewer and storage capabilities as detail enforcement. A successful coverage checkpoint is distinct from business completion, which needs an independently verified artifact or external readback.
