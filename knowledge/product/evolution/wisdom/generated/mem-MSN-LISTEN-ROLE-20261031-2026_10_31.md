# Bridge interactive-CLI and claimed-WorkItem identity with a minimal scoped role

When a perception command fails with permission_denied on tenant scratch, check for write/read grant asymmetry and bridge the interactive CLI identity to the claimed-WorkItem identity with a narrowly scoped role — after human approval and a design note, grant the minimum.

## Trigger Phrases

- MSN-LISTEN-ROLE-20261031 traced a listen failure to asymmetric grants (the tmp floor default_allow covered writes only) and bridged the interactive CLI identity to claimed-WorkItem identity by adding the perception_media_worker role under persisted tenant scope. Lesson: treat a security-boundary change as human approval -> design doc -> minimal grant, and record the authorized runtime probe alongside focused tests so the access boundary stays reviewable.
