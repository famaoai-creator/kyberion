# External actuator approval resume needs persisted control-path simulation

Validate human-to-headless resume with real approval and execution stores while replacing only final transports.

mission

## Trigger Phrases

Assert zero external calls before approval, one after approval, no extra calls on replay, and zero model repair after an ambiguous effect. Review artifact references participate in approval payload binding and must remain deterministic when interactive execution resumes headlessly.
