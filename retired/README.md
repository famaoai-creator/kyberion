# retired/

Code, pipelines and schemas that are no longer part of the active build, kept in the tree so
future contributors can see what existed and why it was removed without digging through old
SHAs. Nothing under `retired/` is built, linted into the runtime, or collected by vitest
(`vitest.config.mts` excludes `**/retired/**`); relative imports inside it are left as they were
and are not expected to resolve.

Area-specific indexes: [`actuators/README.md`](./actuators/README.md),
[`pipelines/README.md`](./pipelines/README.md).

## How to restore

`git mv` the file (and its test) back to its original path, re-add the barrel export
(`libs/core/index-part-*.ts` and the domain `index.ts`) and the `libs/core/package.json`
subpath, re-register any boundary baseline entry listed in the row, and wire a production
caller in the same change — restoring without a caller just recreates the orphan.
